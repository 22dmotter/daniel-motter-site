Hey, I'm Daniel

Just a guy who got tired of owing.

I'm a recent Lee University grad, newly married, and working to get out of debt for good. This channel started as a way to keep myself honest, and it turned into a place where we figure this out together.

I started this because I really wanted to be financially free. I want to help my parents get out of debt, and then one day help others do the same. I have a real passion for helping people get financially free.

"Let no debt remain outstanding, except the continuing debt to love one another."Romans 13:8

I want to be free so we can give generously, live with peace, and steward well what God's given us.
