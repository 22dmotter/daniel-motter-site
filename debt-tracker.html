Your numbers. Your progress.

Track your debt journey.

Keep a record of your real payments and balances. See your progress, recognize milestones, and keep moving forward—one step at a time.

Before you begin

No account or bank connection. This tracker saves only in this browser on this device—not on our server—and does not sync between devices. Anyone using this browser profile can see it. Clearing browser data, private browsing, or blocked storage can lose your progress.

Download backups regularly. Backup files contain unencrypted financial information. Store them somewhere safe. Use debt nicknames, never account numbers, passwords, or other sensitive details.

Net balance reduction = starting balances minus current balances. It can be negative if balances rise. Payments may include interest or fees, so payments logged are not the same as debt reduced. Adding or deleting a debt changes your baseline and progress.

Small wins matter

Your milestones.

These are current statuses, not permanent awards. They use only the debts and history you keep here. Balances rising, adding or deleting a debt, or undoing entries can change or relock them. No earned dates are guessed.

    A debt-payoff milestone requires a recorded balance change from above zero to zero and a current balance of zero. An opening balance of zero alone doesn't count as a recorded payoff.

    Keep it current

    Your debts.

    Use the remaining balance from your lender, including any new interest, fees, or borrowing. Log a payment separately from that balance; this tracker never guesses how much went to principal.

    Add a debt

    Starting partway through? Use the balance when your journey began as your starting balance, then enter the first balance you can verify now. Existing progress counts toward net reduction, but no past payments or events will be invented.

    Dates must be on or before today. The first recorded balance date must be on or after your journey start date. Starting balance must be positive; remaining balance can be zero or higher than the starting balance. Up to 50 debts and 5,000 total history entries; each amount at most $10,000,000.

    Keep a copy

    Back up your journey.

    Download a backup before clearing browser data, switching devices, or making big changes. To move your tracker, import that file in the other browser. There is no automatic sync.

    Backups include nicknames, balances, dates, and payment history in unencrypted JSON. Treat them as private financial files. Import replaces the whole tracker, not merges it, and requires confirmation. Only version 1 tracker backups up to 2 MB are accepted.

    Your current tracker stays unchanged if validation fails or you cancel. Download your current backup before replacing it.

    This removes every debt and entry from this browser's tracker after confirmation. It cannot be undone without a backup.

    This tool is for recording your own information, not financial advice or a lender statement. Check balances with your lender. No interest or payoff-date estimates are calculated here. For planning, use Calculate Your Payment or read the Get Out of Debt guide.
