Getting out of debt

Why debt is bad, and why I'm done with it.

Debt is sold as convenience, but it quietly costs you money, peace, and freedom. Here are seven reasons debt is bad, and why I'm paying off every dollar I owe.

The real cost

7 ways debt steals your freedom.

Reason 1

It makes everything cost more.

Interest means you pay back more than you borrowed. A purchase on a credit card you carry for months or years can end up costing far more than its price tag. You're paying extra for the privilege of having it sooner.

Reason 2

It spends your future paycheck.

Every monthly payment is money you've already promised away before you earn it. The more you owe, the less of your income is actually yours to decide what to do with.

Reason 3

It shrinks your margin.

When payments eat up your budget, there's no room left for surprises. A flat tire or a doctor's bill turns into more debt, and the cycle keeps going.

Reason 4

It adds stress you carry everywhere.

Money worries follow you to work, to bed, and into your relationships. Owing money can quietly weigh on your peace and your marriage.

Reason 5

It limits your choices.

Debt can keep you stuck in a job you'd leave, stop you from taking a risk on something you're called to, and delay big goals like buying a home.

Reason 6

It keeps you from giving.

It's hard to be generous when your money is already spoken for. Getting out of debt frees you to help family, support your church, and bless people in need.

Reason 7

It keeps you from building wealth.

Every dollar going to interest is a dollar not being saved or invested. Paying off debt is the first step to actually growing something for your future.

Faith and money

What the Bible says about debt.

"Let no debt remain outstanding, except the continuing debt to love one another."Romans 13:8

Scripture doesn't treat debt as something to manage forever. It treats it as something to get free of, so the only thing we owe anyone is love. That's the goal I'm chasing: owing nothing, so I'm free to live and give generously.

FAQ

Common questions about debt.

Why is debt bad?

Debt is bad because it commits your future income to paying back the past. Interest makes everything you buy cost more, monthly payments shrink your margin, and owing money limits your freedom to save, give, and make choices.

Is all debt bad?

Some people see a mortgage or student loan as useful, but every debt still carries interest and risk. The less you owe, the more of your paycheck you keep and the more options you have.

What does the Bible say about debt?

Romans 13:8 says, "Let no debt remain outstanding, except the continuing debt to love one another." Scripture treats debt as something to get free of so we can live and give generously.

How do I start getting out of debt?

List every debt with its balance and interest rate, build a simple budget, stop adding new debt, and put every extra dollar toward one debt at a time until it's gone, then roll that payment into the next.
