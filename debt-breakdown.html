How to read this breakdown

Every contribution I record names the debt it went toward. Each debt's paid amount, remaining balance, progress bar and tracker come from the same ledger as my overall total, so the four debts always add up to the total remaining. Starting balances are the amounts before any recorded contributions. Credit Card 2's starting balance includes the contributions from Nov 3–7, 2026, and Credit Card 1's includes the Nov 8, 2026 contribution.

If the figures ever stop reconciling with the overall total, this page will flag that they need updating instead of guessing.
