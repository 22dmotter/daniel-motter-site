Why faith is part of this

Free to give, free to live.

For me, getting out of debt isn't just about money. It's about stewardship: taking care of what God has trusted us with, and not letting what we owe decide what we can do.

"The rich rule over the poor, and the borrower is slave to the lender."Proverbs 22:7

Debt quietly takes away your freedom. Every payment to a lender is a dollar that can't go to my family, my church, or someone in need. I want that freedom back.

"Let no debt remain outstanding, except the continuing debt to love one another."Romans 13:8

That's the goal: owe nothing but love. I'm not here to preach. I'm here to show what it looks like to chase that goal on a real budget, mistakes included.
