Coming Soon

A clearer picture of your finances.

I'm planning a subscription-based application to help people organize their money, understand their spending, and work toward greater financial stability.

An idea in the planning stage.

The app has not launched. There are no signups or subscriptions available right now. Features, pricing, and launch details are still being considered.

Why I'm exploring this

More clarity. A path forward.

Getting out of debt is part of a bigger goal: feeling more in control of your money. I'd like to build something that helps people see where they stand and take manageable steps toward their financial goals.

The intention is to bring everyday money tracking and longer-term goals together in a straightforward tool. It would support your own decisions and habits—not promise financial results.

Planned ideas

What it could help with.

These are ideas I'm considering, not a confirmed feature list. The scope may change as the plans develop.

Organizing your money

The goal would be to help you bring income, spending, and financial priorities into a clearer picture.

Understanding spending

I'd like to make it easier to review where money is going and spot habits you may want to adjust.

Following debt progress

One idea is to help people track debt balances and recognize progress as they work toward paying them down.

Working toward savings goals

Another idea is to help you organize savings goals and keep sight of the steps you're taking toward a more stable financial footing.

The proposed model

A subscription-based app.

A subscription model is planned, but pricing and subscription details have not been set. There's no launch date to announce. More information can be shared as the plans take shape.

This is an introduction to a future idea—not a product you can subscribe to today.

Available today

You can start tracking now.

The existing Debt Tracker is separate from this planned app and works today without a subscription. It keeps your entries in your browser on this device, with a backup download option—not cross-device sync. Keep backups safe; clearing browser data can erase your progress.

Nothing on this page moves or uploads your existing tracker data. You can also follow my own debt-free journey on the Channel page.
