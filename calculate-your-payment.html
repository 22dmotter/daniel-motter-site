Assumptions

How the math works.

Every month

Interest is added at your APR divided by 12 on what you owe, then your payment is made at the end of the month. The rate stays the same, and there are no fees, late charges, or new purchases.

Minimum payments

Credit card minimums usually shrink as your balance drops. This planner keeps the minimum you enter fixed, which is the steady payment you'd need to keep making to stay on this plan.

Snowball vs. avalanche

Each debt gets its minimum first. Your extra money, plus payments freed up from debts you've paid off, goes to the smallest balance (snowball) or the highest APR (avalanche).

Just an estimate

Results are estimates for planning, not financial advice. Your lender's statements are the final word. The planner stops at 50 years and will tell you if your payments don't cover the interest.
